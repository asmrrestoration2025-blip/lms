## Dashboards

Every role receives its own information architecture:

Student:
- learning progress
- enrolled courses
- continue learning
- certificates
- wishlist

Instructor:
- course performance
- enrollments
- revenue
- course management
- reviews

Admin:
- moderation
- users
- courses
- payments
- reports

Superadmin:
- platform health
- admin management
- permissions
- users
- courses
- financial oversight
- audit logs
- settings

## Responsive

Desktop, tablet and mobile must all be supported.
